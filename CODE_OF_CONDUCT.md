# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility and apologizing to those affected by mistakes, and learning from the experience.
- Focusing on what is best for the community, users, and contributors.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, public or private attacks, publishing others’ private information, and other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code of conduct. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this code, and may take other actions they consider appropriate.

## Scope

This code applies within project spaces and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces. It also applies when conduct would create a safety or harassment risk for project participants.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported through the maintainer’s [vincenzo-afk GitHub profile](https://github.com/vincenzo-afk). Reports should include enough context to permit a fair review and should avoid sharing sensitive personal information unnecessarily.

Project maintainers will respect the privacy and security of the reporter when handling an enforcement report.
